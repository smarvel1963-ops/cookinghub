Cooking Hub privacy policy

Cooking Hub is an app by Marvel Corp, part of the Hub family of apps. Last updated November 15, 2026 (sharing recipes added).

The short version

What you put in Cooking Hub stays on your phone. There are no Cooking Hub accounts, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. Your information is never sold and never used for advertising. Two things leave your phone only when you choose them - sharing a recipe with every Cooking Hub, and the optional AI helper - and they are listed below.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type: your first name if you give it, the look you pick, your own recipes (name, category, servings, times, ingredients, directions, the family story and notes, who it came from, and whether it is a Private Family Recipe), drafts, favorites, your pantry (items, amounts, groups and use-by dates), the ingredients you add in the Dinner Finder, your week's dinner plan, your grocery list, the times you cooked a recipe and your own ratings. These are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. We cannot see them.

Photos (a photo of a dish and the photo you take of an old recipe card) are kept in a separate store on your phone. They are never put in the backup and never sent anywhere. Cooking Hub does not read the writing on a card - it keeps the photo, and you type the words.

Deleting: ⚙ Settings → Your data → Erase everything deletes your Cooking Hub information on the phone (photos included). One recipe: open it → Delete.

Not nutrition or health advice

Cooking Hub gives recipes and cooking steps. Safe cooking temperatures in the steps follow the USDA. Nothing in the app is nutrition, diet or health advice.

Recipes from videos

Some built-in recipes are our own versions of dishes seen in creators' videos. Each one links to the original video and names the creator. Tapping Watch the video opens that video on its own site (for example Facebook), under that site's own privacy policy.
