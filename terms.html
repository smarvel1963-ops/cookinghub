Allergens, safe cooking and nutrition

Recipes are cooking ideas and steps, not guarantees. Always check the ingredients for allergens yourself, and check every product label. Cook food to safe temperatures: the safe temperatures in the steps follow the USDA, and a food thermometer is the way to be sure. Any nutrition numbers are estimates. Nothing in Cooking Hub is nutrition, diet or health advice. Some built-in recipes are our own versions of dishes from creators' videos and link to the original video, which opens on its own site. AI helper answers can be wrong, so check anything important.
